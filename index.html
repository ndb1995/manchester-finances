Every dollar of the city budget: revenue sources → funds → spending categories → departments. Hover a band for amounts; click a department for line items.
On the revenue side, comparing a group between years is not strictly like-for-like: the budget books itemize some revenue differently from year to year (the FY2025 book lists recreation revenue that FY2026's does not), so a group can shift because the book changed how it lists a source rather than because the money changed. The spending categories on the right are anchored to the totals the books themselves print and are directly comparable.
